# craft-libs

Vendored at `vendor/craft` (crates `craft-atomic`, `craft-docpath`, `craft-font-catalog`, `craft-font-store`, `craft-fonts-build`, `craft-i18n`, `craft-ui`, and the patched `krilla` 0.8.2). Upstream: craft-libs, dual-licensed MIT OR Apache-2.0.

These files were modified for Wrangl (Apache-2.0 section 4(b)): renamed, trimmed to the parts Wrangl builds, and rebranded.

The Apache License 2.0 text is `vendor/craft/LICENSE-APACHE`.
