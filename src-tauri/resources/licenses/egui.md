# egui

Vendored at `vendor/egui` (ecolor, emath, epaint, epaint_default_fonts, egui, egui-winit, egui-wgpu, egui_extras, eframe 0.37.0 with the fork patches in `vendor/egui/PATCHES.md`). Upstream: https://github.com/emilk/egui, dual-licensed MIT OR Apache-2.0.

These files were modified for Wrangl (Apache-2.0 section 4(b)): renamed, trimmed to the parts Wrangl builds, and rebranded.
